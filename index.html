OUR STORY

Since 1982, a name built on trust.

Founded by Moidu Kuruvattinhalil, Manukkas Jewellery has served customers with prestigious collections for celebrating love, family and personal achievements.

Our dedication to quality and customer satisfaction remains the foundation of our success. We aim to make every visit as memorable as the jewellery itself.

1982

The Inception

Founded on foundational principles of honesty, genuine gold purity, and warm hospitality.

2000s

Expansion Across UAE

Opening flagship doors in Dubai, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, and Sharjah's Al Dhaid.
