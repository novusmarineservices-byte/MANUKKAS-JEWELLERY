# Manukkas Jewellery LLC — Official Luxury Web Experience

**Since 1982 • Crafted with Care • Made for Your Moments**

A digital experience built for **Manukkas Jewellery LLC**, celebrating over 40 years of goldsmithing, heritage collections, and customer dedication across the United Arab Emirates.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Heritage & Royal Aesthetics**: Designed with an obsidian and champagne gold color palette, inspired by high-end luxury houses, featuring Google Fonts *Cinzel*, *Cormorant Garamond*, and *Outfit*.
- **Comprehensive UAE Network**: Interactive locator for all 5 branches with direct phone dialing (`tel:`) and Google Maps navigation:
  1. **Dubai**: International City, Greece Cluster K07, Shop #8 (`04 770 7115`)
  2. **Al Dhaid (Sharjah)**: Opp. Al Ansari Exchange (`06 8827 055`)
  3. **Ajman**: Lucky Round About, Near Al Ansari Exchange (`06 7677 273`)
  4. **Noor Manukkas**: Dhana Complex, Shop 23, Jurf Industrial Zone 1, Opp. Lucky Centre, Ajman (`056 382 0916`)
  5. **Umm Al Quwain**: Nesto Hypermarket (`08 767 5225`)
- **Interactive Collection Showcase**: Filterable categories covering:
  - *Signature* — Timeless Gold
  - *Classic* — Gold Jewellery
  - *Details* — Bracelets & Bangles
  - *Modern* — Delicate Pieces
  - *Everyday* — Pendants
  - *Artisanal* — Jewellery Craftsmanship & Bespoke Creations
- **Interactive Modal & WhatsApp Concierge**: Instant product inquiry modal connecting directly to WhatsApp with pre-composed inquiries.
- **Daily UAE Live Gold Rate Ticker**: Retail market indicators for 24K, 22K, 21K, and 18K in AED per gram.
- **Full Brand Story & Recognition**:
  - Founder tribute to **Moidu Kuruvattinhalil**
  - Accolade spotlight: **Best Jewellery of the Year 2023** awarded by the *Visionary Leader Awards*
- **The 4 Pillars of Excellence**:
  - Expert Craftsmanship
  - Personalised Service
  - Ethical Sourcing
  - Customer Satisfaction
- **Our Services Suite**:
  - 01 Jewellery Design
  - 02 Repair & Maintenance
  - 03 Appraisal Services
  - 04 Trade-In & Buyback
- **Mobile Responsive Drawer & Smooth Interactions**: Optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide screens.
